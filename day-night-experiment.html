🌗 Why We Have Day and Night

Earth spins like a top AND circles the Sun. One of those makes day and night. Which one?

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

🌍 Earth spins

Earth spins around its own axis, like a basketball on a finger — one full spin takes 24 hours.

2

☀️ Only half is lit

The Sun can only shine on the half of Earth facing it. The other half is in darkness.

3

🌅 Spinning = day and night

As Earth spins, your spot rotates into the sunlight (day) then away from it (night) — every 24 hours.

4

🗓️ Orbit makes the year

Earth also travels all the way around the Sun — that full loop takes 365 days and gives us a year, not a day.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: You're not standing still right now — Earth's spin is whizzing you around at about 1,670 km/h at the equator! You don't feel it because everything around you (air included) is moving at the same speed.
