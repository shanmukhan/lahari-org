💉 How Vaccines Work

A vaccine is like a wanted poster for your immune system — showing it the enemy before the real danger ever shows up.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

💉 A safe preview of the germ

A vaccine contains a weakened, dead, or partial piece of a germ — enough to recognize, but not enough to make you sick.

2

🛡️ Your immune system investigates

Your immune system's cells examine this harmless piece and learn exactly what the real germ looks like.

3

🧠 It builds a memory

Special "memory cells" remember this germ's exact shape — like keeping a wanted poster on file, for years.

4

⚡ Instant defense if it ever shows up

If the real germ ever invades later, your immune system recognizes it INSTANTLY and attacks before it can make you sick.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: Without a vaccine, your first encounter with a new germ can take your body about a week to build up a strong defense — plenty of time to get sick. With a vaccine, that "first encounter" already happened safely ahead of time, so your defense is ready in hours, not days!
