🕳️ What Is a Black Hole?

A place where gravity is so strong that not even light can escape. Let's see how one forms.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

⭐ A giant star runs out of fuel

Huge stars spend millions of years burning fuel, fighting their own gravity by pushing outward with heat and light.

2

💥 It collapses inward

Once the fuel runs out, there's nothing left pushing outward — gravity wins, and the star collapses in on itself.

3

🕳️ Matter crushes to a point

For the biggest stars, the collapse doesn't stop — all that mass gets crushed into an unimaginably tiny point.

4

🚫 Not even light escapes

The gravity around that point becomes so strong that within a certain distance (the "event horizon"), nothing — not even light — can escape.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: A black hole isn't a hole at all — there's nothing to fall "into" in the way a hole in the ground works. It's simply a region where so much mass is packed so tightly that gravity bends space itself into a one-way trap.
