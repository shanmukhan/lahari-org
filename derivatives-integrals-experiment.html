📉 Derivatives & Integrals, Visualized

Position, velocity, and acceleration are all linked by the same slope-and-area trick, chained together.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

📍 Position over time

Picture a rocket's height over time — a curve that starts flat, then rises faster and faster.

2

🏎️ Slope of position = velocity

Take the DERIVATIVE (slope) of the position curve at every point, and you get a new curve: the rocket's velocity over time.

3

🚀 Slope of velocity = acceleration

Take the derivative AGAIN, and the velocity curve's slope gives you acceleration — how quickly the speed itself is changing.

4

🔄 Integrate backward to undo it

Going the other way: the AREA under the acceleration curve rebuilds velocity, and the area under velocity rebuilds position — integration undoes differentiation!

🤯 Mind-blowing part: This exact chain — position, velocity, acceleration — is how engineers design everything from roller coasters to Mars landers. NASA's landing sequences are calculated by working with acceleration and integrating (adding up) all the way back to a precise final position, down to the meter.
