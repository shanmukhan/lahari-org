💥 The Big Bang Story

How did EVERYTHING — all space, all matter, all time — get its start? Let's watch 13.8 billion years happen.

STEP 1
Press Play to start the show!
1

💥 A tiny, hot start

13.8 billion years ago, everything we know — all space and matter — was packed into an unimaginably tiny, hot point.

2

🎈 Space itself stretched

It didn't explode "into" space — space itself began expanding, carrying everything along with it, and still is today!

3

⚛️ Particles, then atoms

As it cooled, tiny particles formed, then joined into the first simple atoms — mostly hydrogen and helium.

4

⭐ Stars and galaxies light up

Gravity pulled gas clouds together until they got hot enough to shine — the first stars, then galaxies, were born.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: Every atom in your body — except the hydrogen — was cooked inside a star that exploded billions of years ago. You are, quite literally, made of stardust!
