🚀 Special Relativity — Explained Simply

Light always travels at the same speed, no matter how fast YOU are moving. That one weird fact changes everything.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

🚗 Normal speeds add up

If you throw a ball forward from a moving car, the ball's speed = car's speed + your throw's speed. That's how speed normally works.

2

💡 Light breaks that rule

Einstein realized: no matter how fast you're moving, light always measures at the SAME speed — 300,000 km per second — for everyone.

3

⏱️ Something has to give

For light's speed to stay the same for everyone, something else must change instead — and that something is time itself.

4

🌌 Space and time are linked

Special relativity says space and time are woven together into one thing: spacetime. Moving through one affects the other.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: GPS satellites orbiting Earth actually experience time slightly differently than we do on the ground, because of their speed and altitude. If we didn't correct for relativity, GPS maps would drift off by kilometers within a single day!
