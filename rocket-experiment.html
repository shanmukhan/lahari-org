🚀 How Rockets Work

No wings, no wheels to push against — so how does a rocket move at all? Newton had the answer.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

🔥 Burn fuel, make gas

A rocket engine burns fuel, creating a huge amount of hot, fast-moving gas.

2

⬇️ Push the gas out

That gas is squeezed out of the nozzle at the bottom, blasting downward at incredible speed.

3

⬆️ The rocket gets pushed back

Newton's third law: every push has an equal push back. Pushing gas down pushes the rocket up!

4

🛰️ Staging drops dead weight

As fuel tanks empty, rockets often drop that section entirely — less weight to carry means the rest can go faster.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: Rockets don't need air to push against — that's actually a myth! They work even better in the vacuum of space, since there's no air resistance slowing them down. That's exactly why rockets (not planes) get us to orbit.
🧪 Now try it yourself

Blow up a balloon and let go — the same action-reaction push that launches a rocket!

Air rushes out one way, the balloon zooms the other way!
