🌈 How Rainbows Form

White sunlight secretly contains every color. Raindrops are what split it apart for you to see.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

☀️ Sunlight looks white

Sunlight looks white or yellow, but it's secretly a mix of every color of light blended together.

2

💧 A raindrop bends light

When light enters a round raindrop, it slows down and bends — and each color bends by a slightly different amount.

3

🪞 It bounces off the back

The light reflects off the inside back wall of the raindrop, like a tiny curved mirror, and heads back out.

4

🌈 Colors fan out separately

As it exits, each color bends again by a different amount, so the colors spread apart into a band — red on the outside, violet on the inside.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: No two people ever see the exact same rainbow! Each person's eyes catch light from a slightly different set of raindrops, so the rainbow you see is a personal one, made just for where you're standing.
🧪 Now try it yourself

Drag the slider to change how much each color bends, and watch the rainbow spread apart.

Slide to 0: all colors travel together as "white" light.
