🌱 How Photosynthesis Works

Plants don't eat food — they build their own, out of sunlight, air, and water. Here's the recipe.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

☀️ Sunlight hits the leaf

Leaves are packed with a green pigment called chlorophyll, which captures energy from sunlight.

2

💧 Water travels up from roots

Roots soak up water from the soil, which travels all the way up the stem into the leaves.

3

💨 CO₂ enters through tiny pores

Tiny pores on the leaf's underside, called stomata, let carbon dioxide from the air in.

4

🍬 Sunlight + water + CO₂ = sugar + oxygen

Using the sun's energy, the plant combines water and CO₂ into sugar (its food) — and releases oxygen as a bonus!

🤯 Mind-blowing part: Almost every breath of oxygen you've ever taken was made by a plant (or ocean algae) doing exactly this. Photosynthesis is the reason Earth's air is breathable at all — plants have been quietly making our oxygen for billions of years!
