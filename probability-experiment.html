🎲 Probability & Statistics Basics

A coin flip is 50/50 no matter what — but watch what happens the more times you flip it.

Flip a coin many times and watch the results settle down.
1

🎲 Probability = a fraction of outcomes

Probability measures how likely something is — a coin has 2 equally likely sides, so heads has a probability of 1 in 2.

2

🪙 A few flips can be lopsided

Flip a coin just 5 times, and it's totally normal to get 4 heads and 1 tail — small samples can look "unfair" by chance.

3

📊 More flips settle toward 50/50

Flip it 1,000 times, and the results settle very close to 50% heads, 50% tails — this is called the "Law of Large Numbers."

4

📈 Statistics: making sense of data

Statistics is the toolbox for summarizing all that data — averages, spread, and patterns — instead of getting lost in individual results.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: This is exactly why casinos always make money in the long run, even though any single player CAN win — each game has odds slightly in the casino's favor, and over millions of plays, the Law of Large Numbers guarantees the casino comes out ahead, even though no one can predict any single spin or hand.
