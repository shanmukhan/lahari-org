📶 How Wi-Fi Works

There's no wire — so how does a video stream from a box in your hallway to a tablet across the house?

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

📁 Data becomes 1s and 0s

Whatever you're sending — a photo, a message — first gets turned into a long string of 1s and 0s: binary code.

2

📡 Radio waves carry it

Your router turns those 1s and 0s into invisible radio waves — the same kind of wave as FM radio, just a different frequency.

3

🌊 Waves spread through the air

Those radio waves ripple outward through your home in every direction, right through walls (though they weaken passing through).

4

📲 Your device decodes them

Your phone or laptop's antenna picks up the waves and translates them back into the original 1s and 0s — and your video plays!

🤯 Mind-blowing part: Wi-Fi radio waves are just very high-frequency cousins of the light you see — they're the exact same kind of thing (electromagnetic waves) as visible light, radio, and X-rays. Wi-Fi just uses a frequency your eyes can't detect!
