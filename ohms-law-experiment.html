🔋 Ohm's Law & Circuits

Voltage, current, and resistance are all linked by one simple equation that runs every circuit you own.

Adjust voltage and resistance and watch the current change.
1

🔋 Voltage: the push

Voltage is the "electrical pressure" pushing electrons through a circuit — more voltage means a stronger push.

2

🚧 Resistance: the friction

Resistance is how much a material fights the flow of electrons — thin wires and certain materials resist more than others.

3

➗ Current = Voltage ÷ Resistance

Ohm's Law: I = V/R. More push (voltage) makes more current; more friction (resistance) makes less current.

4

💡 Every circuit obeys this

From a flashlight to a supercomputer, every simple circuit follows this same relationship between voltage, current, and resistance.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: This is exactly why a wall socket (high voltage) can be dangerous through your body (low resistance, since you're full of salty water) but a 9-volt battery touched to your tongue just tingles — the resistance and voltage together determine how much current actually flows through you, and current is what matters for a shock.
