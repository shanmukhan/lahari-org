🔬 Dissecting an Atom

Click each part of the atom below to pull it apart, layer by layer, and see what's really inside.

Click on a part of the atom (or a card below) to explore it!

🖱️ Click the electrons, the nucleus, or a proton/neutron to zoom in

•

⚛️ The whole atom

Mostly empty space! If the nucleus were the size of a marble, the electrons would orbit almost a kilometer away.

1

⚡ Electrons

Tiny, negatively-charged particles that zoom around the nucleus in a fuzzy cloud — they're what makes chemistry happen.

2

🎯 The nucleus

The dense center, made of protons and neutrons packed tightly together, held by the strong nuclear force.

3

🔴 Protons

Positively-charged particles. The number of protons decides which element it is (1 = hydrogen, 6 = carbon...).

4

⚪ Neutrons

No electric charge. They add mass and help hold the nucleus together without pushing protons apart.

5

🔸 Quarks (inside protons)

Zoom in even further: protons and neutrons are each built from 3 tinier particles called quarks!

🤯 Mind-blowing part: An atom is almost entirely empty space. If you removed all the empty space from every atom in every human on Earth, all 8 billion of us would fit into a space smaller than a sugar cube!
