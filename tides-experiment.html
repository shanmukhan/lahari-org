🌊 What Causes Tides

The Moon's gravity gently tugs on Earth's oceans — and that tug is why beaches flood and drain every single day.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

🌕 The Moon pulls on Earth

Just like Earth pulls on the Moon, the Moon's gravity also pulls on Earth — including its oceans.

2

🌊 Water bulges toward it

Ocean water on the side facing the Moon gets pulled up into a bulge — that's high tide there.

3

🌊 And bulges on the far side too!

Surprisingly, water on the OPPOSITE side also bulges — because the solid Earth gets pulled toward the Moon slightly more than the far water does, leaving it behind.

4

🔄 Earth spins through both bulges

As Earth rotates every 24 hours, each coastline passes through both bulges — giving most places two high tides and two low tides a day.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: The Sun pulls on our oceans too — with less force than the Moon, but when Sun, Moon, and Earth line up (full or new moon), their pulls team up for extra-strong "spring tides." When they're at right angles, tides are weaker — "neap tides."
