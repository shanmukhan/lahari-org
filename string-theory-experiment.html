🎻 String Theory — Explained Simply

What if every tiny particle in the universe is really a tiny, vibrating string — like a guitar string?

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

🔎 Zoom into an atom

An atom has a nucleus (protons, neutrons) and electrons. But protons and neutrons are made of even smaller bits: quarks.

2

🎸 What if quarks are strings?

String theory guesses that quarks and electrons aren't tiny dots — they're impossibly tiny vibrating strings of energy.

3

🎵 Different vibration = different particle

Just like a guitar string makes different notes depending on how it vibrates, a tiny string makes a different particle depending on how IT vibrates.

4

🌌 One idea, everything explained?

Scientists like this idea because ONE kind of tiny string, vibrating differently, could explain ALL particles and even gravity — a "theory of everything."

🤯 Mind-blowing part: String theory says these strings might vibrate in more directions than we can see — maybe 10 dimensions in total, with extra ones curled up so tiny we'd never notice them, like a hidden fold in space itself. Nobody has proven this yet — it's still one of science's biggest unsolved puzzles!
