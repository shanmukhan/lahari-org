👁️ How Your Eye Works

Your eye is basically a camera your body grew — with a lens, a light-sensing screen, and a cable straight to your brain.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

⚫ Light enters through the pupil

Light enters through the pupil, the dark opening in the center — the colored iris around it widens or shrinks the pupil to control how much light gets in.

2

🔍 The lens focuses it

Just behind the pupil, a flexible lens bends the incoming light, focusing it onto the back of the eye.

3

📺 The retina senses it — upside down!

The image lands on the retina, packed with light-sensing cells — but because of how lenses work, the image lands UPSIDE DOWN.

4

🧠 The brain flips it right-side up

The retina sends signals down the optic nerve to your brain, which automatically flips the image the right way up — so you never notice!

🤯 Mind-blowing part: Everyone's eyes see the world upside down on their retina — your brain has been silently flipping it right-side up your whole life. Scientists have even had volunteers wear glasses that flip everything upside down, and after about a week wearing them constantly, their brains adapted and started seeing normally again!
