⚙️ How a DC Motor Works

From magnets, to electricity making magnetism, to a coil that spins forever — step by step.

Press Play to start the show!
1

🧲 Magnets have poles

Every magnet has a NORTH and a SOUTH end. Opposite poles attract, same poles repel.

2

⚡ Electricity → magnetism

Current flowing through a wire makes its own magnetic field. Coil the wire and the field gets strong — an electromagnet!

3

👉 A push is born

Put that coil between a magnet's N and S poles, and each side of the coil gets pushed — it starts to turn.

4

🔁 The commutator trick

A commutator flips the current every half-turn, so the push always spins the coil the SAME way — forever.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: without the commutator, the coil would just wobble back and forth and stop. That tiny flipping trick is the difference between a wobble and a motor spinning thousands of times a minute!
🧪 Now try it yourself

Flip the commutator switch on and off and watch what happens to the coil.
