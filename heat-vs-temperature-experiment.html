🌡️ Heat vs Temperature

A cup of tea and a swimming pool can be the exact same temperature — but they hold wildly different amounts of heat.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

🌡️ Temperature: how fast, on average

Temperature measures the AVERAGE speed of molecules jiggling around — it doesn't care how many molecules there are.

2

🔥 Heat: total energy

Heat is the TOTAL energy of all those jiggling molecules added together — it very much depends on how much stuff there is.

3

☕ Same temperature, different heat

A cup of 90°C tea and a bathtub of 90°C water are the same temperature — but the bathtub has vastly more total heat energy, because it has vastly more molecules.

4

🥶 That's why the ocean cools slowly

The ocean holds so much heat energy that it takes a huge amount of cooling to drop its temperature even slightly — which is why it warms and cools land nearby.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: A single lit match reaches a higher TEMPERATURE (over 600°C) than a giant iceberg's surface, sure — but that entire iceberg holds astronomically more total HEAT energy than the match. If you dropped the iceberg into a bathtub of match-hot water, the iceberg would barely feel it — but a tub full of icebergs would freeze that match's heat away instantly!
