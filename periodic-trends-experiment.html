📊 Periodic Trends

The periodic table isn't just a list — its layout hides patterns you can read at a glance.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

⚛️ Atomic size shrinks left-to-right

Moving left to right across a row, atoms get SMALLER — each extra proton pulls the electron cloud in tighter.

2

📏 Atomic size grows top-to-bottom

Moving down a column, atoms get BIGGER — each row adds a whole new electron shell further out.

3

🧲 Electronegativity grows left-to-right

Elements on the right (like oxygen, fluorine) pull electrons toward themselves strongly — that pulling power is called electronegativity.

4

↘️ The strongest pullers are top-right

Combine both trends: the smallest, most electron-hungry atoms cluster in the top-right corner — fluorine is the single most electronegative element.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: These trends aren't random — they come straight from physics. More protons pull electrons in tighter (shrinking atoms), while more electron shells push the "edge" farther out (growing atoms). Once you see the pattern, you can predict properties of an element you've never even studied, just from its position on the table!
