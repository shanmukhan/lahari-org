About

Building things, one problem at a time.

I care more about whether something is genuinely useful than whether it's technically impressive. Every product I've shipped started the same way — noticing a real, specific annoyance and deciding to just go fix it.

I design, code, and ship everything myself — from the idea to the backend to the app in your hand — and I run the infrastructure myself too. Nothing here sits behind a big cloud provider; it's all served from a small computer at home, which I like precisely because it forces everything to stay simple and honest.

Journey

How this came together.

  • The starting point

    Started building solo.

    Decided to build products end-to-end myself rather than wait for the "right team" — idea, code, design, and ship.

  • Shipping

    Turned ideas into six live products.

    SparkCards, EllieEats, Nirvana, VaultZero, NexaLink, and more — each one built around a real problem, not a technology looking for a use.

  • Infrastructure

    Brought it all home — literally.

    Moved hosting onto a Raspberry Pi at home: Apache, TLS, reverse proxies, systemd services, all self-managed instead of rented.

  • Today

    This page.

    A small personal home base at lahari.org — not another product, just a place that's simply, quietly mine.
