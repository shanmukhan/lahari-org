🧊 States of Matter

Ice, water, and steam are the exact same substance — only how fast its molecules move is different.

Solid: molecules vibrate in place, locked in a tight pattern.
Temperature: freezing cold
1

🧊 Solid: locked in place

In a solid, molecules are packed tightly and only vibrate in place — they don't have enough energy to move around.

2

💧 Liquid: free to slide

Add heat and molecules gain enough energy to slide past each other, while still staying close together — that's a liquid.

3

💨 Gas: flying free

Add even more heat and molecules break completely free, flying around fast and spreading out to fill any space.

4

🌡️ It's all about energy

Nothing about the molecules themselves changes — only how much energy (heat) they have, which controls how much they move.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: There's a fourth common state called plasma — add even more energy than a gas has, and electrons get ripped clean off their atoms. Stars, lightning, and neon signs are all glowing plasma!
