🔬 Cell Division — Mitosis & Meiosis

Every cell in your body came from one cell splitting, over and over. There are two very different ways it happens.

Pick mitosis or meiosis to see how it works.
1

🔵 Mitosis: exact copies

Most of your cells divide by mitosis — one cell copies its DNA and splits into two IDENTICAL cells, for growth and repair.

2

🟠 Meiosis: making sex cells

Meiosis is different — it makes sperm or egg cells, dividing TWICE to make four cells with HALF the DNA.

3

➗ Why half the DNA matters

When a sperm (half DNA) meets an egg (half DNA), they combine to make a full set again — that's how you got exactly half your DNA from each parent!

4

🔀 Meiosis also shuffles genes

During meiosis, chromosomes swap little pieces with each other — which is a big reason siblings all look different from each other.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: Your body performs mitosis roughly 2 trillion times a day just to replace worn-out cells — skin, blood, and gut lining cells especially. You are, quite literally, rebuilding yourself constantly!
