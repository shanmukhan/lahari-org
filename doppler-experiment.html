🚨 The Doppler Effect

Why does an ambulance siren sound higher as it approaches, then suddenly drop lower as it passes?

Press Play to hear (see) it happen!
1

〰️ Sound waves spread as rings

A moving siren sends out sound waves in circles — like ripples from a stone dropped in a pond.

2

➡️ Moving forward squeezes the waves

Since the ambulance moves forward between each wave, the waves ahead of it get squeezed closer together.

3

🎵 Squeezed waves = higher pitch

Closer-together waves reach your ear more often per second — and more waves per second sounds like a HIGHER pitch.

4

📉 Behind it, waves stretch out

Behind the ambulance, waves get stretched farther apart — fewer per second reaches your ear, sounding LOWER — that's the drop you hear as it passes!

🤯 Mind-blowing part: This same effect works with LIGHT, not just sound! Stars and galaxies moving away from us stretch their light toward red ("redshift"), and this is one of the key pieces of evidence that the whole universe is expanding.
