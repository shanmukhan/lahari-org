🪐 How Gravity Works

Why do things fall down? And why would you feel so much lighter on the Moon?

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

🌍 Every mass pulls

Anything with mass — you, a ball, a planet — pulls on everything else with mass. Bigger mass = bigger pull.

2

⬇️ Earth pulls hardest

Earth is huge, so it pulls you down with a strong force. That's the "weight" you feel.

3

🌕 Smaller world, weaker pull

The Moon has far less mass than Earth, so its gravity is only about 1/6th as strong.

4

🔴 Mars is in between

Mars is bigger than the Moon but smaller than Earth, so its gravity pull sits in the middle — about 38% of Earth's.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: Your mass never changes — you're made of the same amount of "stuff" everywhere. But your weight changes, because weight is just how hard gravity pulls on your mass! A 60 kg astronaut weighs like 60 kg on Earth, but only like 10 kg on the Moon.
