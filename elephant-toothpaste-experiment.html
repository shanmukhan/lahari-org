🧪 Elephant Toothpaste

A catalyst, a fizz, and a giant foam explosion — let's see chemistry in action like a video!

Press Play to start the show!
1

🧴 The mix

We start with hydrogen peroxide (like a strong version of the stuff in your first-aid kit), some dish soap, and a squirt of food coloring.

2

⚡ Add the catalyst

A pinch of dry yeast goes in. A catalyst is something that speeds up a reaction WITHOUT being used up itself.

3

💨 Oxygen bubbles

The yeast makes the peroxide break apart super fast into water + oxygen gas. The soap traps all that gas into millions of tiny bubbles — foam!

4

🔥 It gets warm!

This reaction is exothermic — it releases heat. If you touched the bottle right after, it would feel warm!

🤯 Mind-blowing part: the yeast comes out of this reaction completely unchanged — that's exactly what makes it a catalyst. Real demos with strong peroxide can shoot foam several feet in the air, hot to the touch!
