🧪 Hydrogen vs Helium Atoms

The two simplest atoms in the universe — and the two that filled it first. Let's look inside both.

1

⚛️ Hydrogen: the simplest atom

Just 1 proton in the nucleus and 1 electron zipping around it. No neutrons in the most common version. It's the lightest, simplest element that exists.

2

⚛️ Helium: one step up

2 protons AND 2 neutrons packed into the nucleus, with 2 electrons circling outside. It's the second-simplest and second-lightest element.

3

🎈 Why helium floats

Helium atoms are much lighter than the nitrogen and oxygen molecules that make up air, so a balloon full of helium floats upward.

4

🌌 Made in the Big Bang

Hydrogen and helium were the very first atoms to form in the early universe, before any stars existed.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: Helium is named after Helios, the Greek word for the Sun — because it was discovered in sunlight (using a special instrument) in 1868, a full 27 years before it was ever found on Earth!
