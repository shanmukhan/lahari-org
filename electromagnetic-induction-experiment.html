⚡ Electromagnetic Induction

Move a magnet near a wire, and electricity appears — no battery needed. This is how almost all power plants work.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

🧲 A magnet near a coil

Take a coil of wire and a magnet — on their own, nothing happens, even sitting right next to each other.

2

🏃 Move the magnet

But slide the magnet in or out of the coil, and something amazing happens: current starts flowing through the wire!

3

🔄 Changing field = current

It's not the magnet's presence that matters — it's the CHANGE in magnetic field passing through the coil that pushes electrons to move.

4

🏭 Power plants use this everywhere

Spin a magnet continuously inside a coil (using steam, wind, or falling water), and you get continuous electricity — that's a generator!

🤯 Mind-blowing part: Nearly all electricity on Earth — from coal, nuclear, hydro, and wind power plants — is made this exact same way: something spins a magnet near a coil of wire. Solar panels are one of the few major exceptions that make electricity differently!
