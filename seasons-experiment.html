🍂 Why We Have Seasons

It's NOT because Earth gets closer to or farther from the Sun. The real reason is a tilt.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

❌ It's not distance

Earth's orbit is almost a perfect circle — it's barely closer to the Sun in any season. That's not the reason.

2

📐 Earth spins on a tilt

Earth's axis is tilted about 23.5° — it doesn't stand up straight as it circles the Sun.

3

☀️ Tilt means direct or slanted light

When your hemisphere tilts TOWARD the Sun, sunlight hits more directly and days are longer — summer! Tilt away, and light comes in at a slant — winter.

4

🔄 Opposite hemispheres, opposite seasons

While the Northern Hemisphere tilts toward the Sun (summer there), the Southern Hemisphere tilts away (winter there) — at the very same time!

🤯 Mind-blowing part: Earth is actually slightly CLOSER to the Sun during Northern Hemisphere winter (early January) than during summer! Distance barely matters — it's really all about that 23.5° tilt.
