🧬 What Is DNA?

A twisted ladder of instructions, packed inside nearly every cell in your body, spelling out how to build YOU.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

🪜 A twisted ladder

DNA is shaped like a ladder that's been twisted into a spiral — scientists call this a "double helix."

2

🔤 Four letter "rungs"

Each rung of the ladder is made of two matching chemical letters: A pairs with T, and C pairs with G.

3

📖 Long sequences spell out genes

Long stretches of these letters, in a certain order, form "genes" — instructions for building specific things, like eye color.

4

🏗️ Cells read it to build proteins

Your cells constantly read these instructions to build proteins — the building blocks that make your whole body work.

🤯 Mind-blowing part: If you uncoiled all the DNA in just ONE of your cells and stretched it out, it would be about 2 meters long. You have trillions of cells — stretch out all your DNA together, and it would reach to the Sun and back many times over!
