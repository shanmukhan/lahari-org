✈️ How Airplanes Fly

A 300-ton plane, held up by nothing but moving air. Here's the shape that makes it possible.

STEP 1
Press Play to start!
1

🪽 A curved wing shape

An airplane wing is curved on top and flatter on the bottom — that special shape is called an airfoil.

2

💨 Air splits around it

As the plane moves forward, air splits — some flows over the curved top, some flows under the flatter bottom.

3

⬇️ Top air pushes down less

Air moving over the curved top travels a longer path and ends up at lower pressure than the air below — pushing down less.

4

⬆️ Higher pressure below lifts it up

Higher pressure underneath pushes up harder than the lower pressure above pushes down — that pressure difference is lift!

🤯 Mind-blowing part: Wings also work upside-down (like in an airshow stunt) if the wing is angled correctly — lift comes mainly from the wing's angle pushing air downward, not just its curved shape. Airplanes are more clever than a simple curve!
